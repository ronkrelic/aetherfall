The Terraces of Aetherfall

A fallen world.
A new awakening.

Set inside The Terraces of Aetherfall, a colossal stepped world where ancient sky-cities once tumbled into vast, sunlit canyons and glowing subterranean basins, players explore from a tactile first-person perspective.

Every hand, tool, spell, and shield is physically visible in front of you—allowing you to grapple across chasms, vent pressurized steam to launch upward, read physical compass-glyphs on your wrist, and fight alongside dozens of other players in real time.

01 / Traverse

Explore in every direction

Grapple across ruins, ride updrafts with a Glider-Cloak, and steam-boost between four interconnected vertical tiers.

02 / Engage

Break their defenses

Aim every swing, time your blocks, and coordinate Active Stagger breaks to expose enemy weak points.

03 / Converge

Restore the landmarks

Join dozens of Wayfinders in Harmonic Convergences, stabilizing failing landmarks against corrupted surges.

04 / Descend

Delve together

Enter The Sunken Observatory with five players. Traverse split paths, solve resonance puzzles, and face Orrery Warden Vaelis.
