# AETHERFALL: The Sunken Tiers - Races, Classes & Tactile Tools

## 5. Playable Races

- **Terrans (Stone-Kin):** Sturdy, grounded people with subtle mineral striations along their skin or knuckles; naturally resilient to knockback, heat, and heavy loads.
- **Sylphari (Wind-Touched):** Tall, agile folk with faintly luminous markings and light frames; excel at high-altitude movement, glide efficiency, and stamina recovery.
- **Dusk-Born (Canyon-Folk):** Adaptable dwellers of the lower tiers with sharp low-light vision and an intuitive feel for acoustic and magnetic cues in caves and ruins.
- **Clock-Forged (Awakened Automata):** Sentient beings of bronze, ceramic, and living wood powered by a steady core; resistant to toxins and hazards, though vulnerable to extreme electrical/Aether overload if unshielded.

## 6. Classes (Designed for First-Person Play)

Rather than locking players into rigid roles that feel awkward in first-person, each class shapes your primary signature action, movement feel, and group synergy:

- **Vanguard:** Frontline protector wielding heavy shields, tower-warding sigils, and kinetic slams to draw aggro, block wide attacks, and create safe zones for allies.
- **Arcanist:** Channels Aether through focus-staves or gauntlets to cast elemental beams, gravity wells, and healing/shielding wards - rewarding resource management and positioning.
- **Waystalker:** Long-range and mid-range skirmisher using precision bows/crossbows, traps, and mobility dashes to pick off priority targets and scout ahead.
- **Artificer:** Gadget and support specialist deploying turrets, steam-boosters, repair drones, and alchemical buffs to control space and sustain groups in tough fights.

## 7. Tactile First-Person Item Ideas

Because you see your hands and gear constantly in first-person, items should feel physical, functional, and expressive:

- **Brass-and-Oak Grapple Gauntlet:** Fires a cable-hook into cliff ledges, ruins, or large enemies to pull yourself across gaps or yank lightweight foes off balance.
- **Runic Choir Lantern:** A handheld lantern whose glass shifts color and chime-pitch near hidden caches, unstable Aether rifts, or cloaked threats - and can be raised to calm skittish wildlife.
- **Geyser-Flask (Steam/Aether Booster):** A belt-mounted canister that vents a burst of pressurized vapor on demand for emergency vertical hops or mid-air directional nudges.
- **Resonance Tuning Fork:** A utility/combat tool that rings with harmonic waves to shatter crystalline shields, unjam ancient doors, or pulse a brief protective rhythm to nearby teammates.
- **Glider-Cloak:** Deploys from your shoulders when leaping from high ledges, letting you ride updrafts across canyons while scanning landmarks below.
