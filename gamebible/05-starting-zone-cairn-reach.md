# AETHERFALL: The Sunken Tiers - Starting Zone & Opening Questline

This document covers the starting town (Cairn-Reach) and the Opening Questline (Levels 1-10), built to naturally teach first-person movement, Stagger/Breach combat, Aether-tools, and multiplayer public events within the first hour of play.

## Starting Zone & Town: Cairn-Reach (The Amber Steppe Rim)

### Town Overview & Atmosphere

Perched on the sun-warmed stone lip where The Canopy Reach steps down into The Amber Steppe, Cairn-Reach is a bustling frontier settlement built into the ribs of a half-buried ancient aqueduct. Windmills turn brass chimes above timber-and-stone roofs, water cascades from the upper aqueduct arches into terraced pools, and Wayfinders gather around the central square before descending into the wilds.

### Districts & Key Landmarks

- **The Aqueduct Plaza (Central Hub):** Houses the main Waystone Teleport Anchor, the Concordat & Bounty Board, and The Copper Flagon tavern (rested XP hearth, food/drink vendors, and local rumors).
- **The Brass-Root Yard (Crafting & Tool Wing):** Open-air forges, workbenches, and Aether-tuning tables run by Archivist Oakhaven and Master Smith Toren.
- **The Warden's Overlook (Training & Outpost):** A clifftop wooden platform overlooking the steppe where Quartermaster Mira Vance drills new recruits on grapple lines, glider updrafts, and Stagger dummies.
- **The Archway Terraces (Starter Sanctums):** A series of carved stone cliff-alcoves just east of the plaza where players unlock their first Wayfinder Sanctum.

## Opening Questline: "Echoes in the Amber" (Levels 1-10)

The opening arc spans five tightly paced chapters that introduce every core gameplay system through hands-on first-person action rather than text walls.

### Act I: The Flickering Band (Levels 1-2)

**Setting:** A collapsed watchtower trail just above Cairn-Reach.
**Premise:** Your character awakens after a sudden Aether pulse knocks out your caravan's sky-skiff. Your wrist-mounted Resonance Band is cracked and humming erratically.

**Key Beats & Mechanics Taught:**

- **First-Person Movement & Sightlines:** Follow the chimes of a Runic Choir Lantern through mossy ruins to locate surviving scout Kaelen.
- **Basic Combat & Blocking:** Defend Kaelen from two scavenging Cinder-Stalkers, practicing Light Attacks, Heavy Attacks, and timed Right-Click blocks.
- **Arrival at Cairn-Reach:** Reach the Warden's Overlook and receive a formal welcome from Quartermaster Mira Vance, unlocking the town map and fast-travel Waystone.

### Act II: Tools of the Terrace (Levels 2-4)

**Setting:** The Warden's Overlook and the sunken aqueduct arches below town.
**Premise:** Before Mira clears you for open-tier contracts, you must calibrate your Resonance Band and prove you can navigate vertical terrain.

**Key Beats & Mechanics Taught:**

- **The Grapple Gauntlet:** Archivist Oakhaven repairs your wrist band and gifts you a starter Brass-and-Oak Grapple Gauntlet. You test it by grappling across three broken aqueduct pillars to recover stolen brass relay coils.
- **The Active Stagger System (A.S.S.):** In the sunken courtyard below, a dormant Training Construct wakes up. You must use Heavy Attacks and a Resonance Tuning Fork pulse to fill its Stagger Meter, trigger a Breach, and land an E Execute finish.
- **Glider-Cloak Leap:** Climb to the top of the aqueduct arch and deploy your starter Glider-Cloak on an updraft to glide smoothly back into Cairn-Reach's plaza.

### Act III: Smoke on the Steppe (Levels 4-6)

**Setting:** The Amber Steppe Borderlands (open world).
**Premise:** Smoke rises from Sun-Split Quarry, a vital bronze and amber mine supplying Cairn-Reach. The Ash-Crown Syndicate has moved in with portable drill-rigs, agitating the local wildlife and waking ancient constructs.

**Key Beats & Mechanics Taught:**

- **Gathering & Crafting Loop:** Harvest Amber-Veined Slate and Canopy Pine along the road, then forge your first Socketed Weapon and Geyser-Flask at the Brass-Root Yard.
- **Tactical Encounters:** Infiltrate Sun-Split Quarry, using your Geyser-Flask double-jump to flank Syndicate Breach-Troopers on high scaffolding and destroy two drill-beacons.
- **Mini-Boss Fight - Foreman Vane:** Fight a Syndicate lieutenant wearing a prototype Aether-shield; players must stagger him when he vents steam, breaking his shield before finishing him.

### Act IV: A Roof Over the Canyon (Levels 6-8)

**Setting:** The Archway Terraces (Cairn-Reach).
**Premise:** Impressed by your work at the quarry, Quartermaster Mira Vance awards you the deed to a ruined cliffside alcove - your first Wayfinder Sanctum - and introduces you to civic life in Aetherfall.

**Key Beats & Mechanics Taught:**

- **Claiming & Decorating:** Clear rubble from your stone archway, place a Hearth Lantern, a Workbench, and an Archive Chest in first-person, and experience the Rested Resonance buff.
- **Guilds & Group Roles:** Visit the Concordat Hall in town to inspect guild charters, sign up for a Dungeon Delve or Public Bounty, and unlock your first Class Mastery Branch point.

### Act V: The First Convergence (Levels 8-10 - Finale)

**Setting:** The Singing Pillars (a massive ancient resonator ring on the edge of the Gloom-Basin).
**Premise:** The Ash-Crown Syndicate attempts to rip a core straight out of the Singing Pillars, triggering a volatile Harmonic Convergence that threatens to collapse the cliffside beneath Cairn-Reach.

**Key Beats & Mechanics Taught:**

- **Seamless Multiplayer Event:** All nearby players in the zone receive a sky-beam alert and converge on the Singing Pillars together.
- **Multi-Stage Public Boss - The Fractured Colossus:** An ancient Amber Sentinel awakens in a frenzy. Players work together - grappling onto elevated ledges, shattering its glowing leg joints to trigger coordinated Breaches, and channeling Resonance Bands at three perimeter pylons to stabilize the rift.
- **Act Finale & Tier Unlock:** With the Colossus pacified and the rift sealed, Archivist Oakhaven unlocks the Gloom-Basin Lift, opening the bioluminescent lower tier of the world for full exploration, delves, and open-world progression.

## Summary of New-Player Progression Milestones (Levels 1-10)

| Level | Milestone Unlocked | System Introduced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Resonance Band & Choir Lantern | First-person exploration, light/heavy attacks, blocking |
| 3 | Grapple Gauntlet & Glider-Cloak | Vertical traversal, Stagger/Breach combat, E Executes |
| 5 | Geyser-Flask & Weapon Sockets | Gathering, crafting, modular gear tuning, double-jump mobility |
| 7 | Wayfinder Sanctum & Class Mastery | Personal cliffside housing, Rested XP, specialization trees |
| 10 | Gloom-Basin Access & Spire Key | Large-scale Harmonic Convergences, group delves, Tier 2 biome |
