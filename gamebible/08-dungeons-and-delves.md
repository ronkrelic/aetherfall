# AETHERFALL: The Sunken Tiers - Dungeons & Delves

This document details the first 5-player Dungeon Delve for Aetherfall: The Sunken Tiers: The Sunken Observatory, designed for Levels 10–15 at the transition between The Amber Steppe and The Gloom-Basin.

## Dungeon Delve: The Sunken Observatory (5-Player / Levels 10–15)

### Overview & Theme

Built into a flooded, half-submerged spire-dome along the edge of The Gloom-Basin, The Sunken Observatory once tracked celestial Aether tides across the Seven Spires. Half of its bronze lens-rings now rest in shallow glowing water and fungal terraces, while its upper galleries remain dry—requiring a 5-player party to split up, coordinate first-person grappling and gliding, time Active Stagger System (A.S.S.) breaks, and solve environmental resonance puzzles.

- **Recommended Group:** 5 Wayfinders (any mix of Vanguard, Arcanist, Waystalker, and Artificer; at least one stagger-heavy or shield-breaking tool recommended).
- **Estimated Run Time:** 30–40 minutes.
- **Key Mechanics Tested:** Split-path vertical traversal, Resonance Tuning Fork frequency matching, Breach interrupts, and multi-phase boss positioning.

## 1. Room-by-Room Layout & Puzzles

### Room 1: The Flooded Cloister (Entry & Tide-Gate Puzzle)

**Setting:** A grand colonnade with knee-deep bioluminescent water, sunken stone benches, and a heavy brass flood-gate blocking the inner stair.

**Encounter:** Two Blight-Weavers hovering above waterlogged pillars, supported by a pack of Cinder-Stalkers prowling the walkways.

**Puzzle – The Three Tides:**

- Three sunken Resonance Obelisks sit around the cloister, each humming at a distinct pitch when struck with a weapon or Resonance Tuning Fork (Low Amber, Mid Turquoise, High Violet).
- Above the flood-gate, three glowing chime-rings show the required sequence—dynamically randomized per run and hinted by floating water-ripples and lantern chimes.
- **Solution:** Two players use Grapple Gauntlets to reach high ledges and call out the chime symbols while the party clears Stalkers and strikes the three Obelisks in order within 12 seconds to raise the flood-gate.

### Room 2: The Pendulum Gallery (Vertical Gauntlet & Beam Puzzle)

**Setting:** A tall, open atrium featuring swinging bronze pendulums, broken sky-bridges, and three rotating Star-Mirrors mounted on stone pedestals.

**Encounter:** Syndicate Breach-Troopers stationed on elevated catwalks with laser-sighted Aether rifles, plus patrolling Amber Sentinel ward-drones.

**Puzzle – Aligning the Star-Beam:**

- A ceiling aperture projects a shaft of golden-blue Aether light into the room. To unseal the Observatory Vault door, the beam must bounce through all three Star-Mirrors and hit the Sun-Dial Receptor on the far balcony.
- Each Star-Mirror can be rotated in first-person (E to turn 45°) and unlocked by clearing its guarding Breach-Trooper squad.
- **Solution:** Players use Geyser-Flasks and Glider-Cloaks to cross gaps between catwalks, angle the mirrors while dodging sniper lines, and lock the beam into the Sun-Dial to drop the vault bridge.

### Room 3: The Mycelial Cistern (Split-Level Stagger Trial)

**Setting:** A dimly lit sunken chamber divided into a Lower Fungal Basin (spore hazards and Blight-Weavers) and an Upper Brass Catwalk (mechanized lock-levers).

**Encounter:** Two Amber Sentinels guarding twin Aether Relays that power a barrier across the exit archway.

**Puzzle – Twin Resonance:**

- The two Amber Sentinels are linked by a glowing tether: if one enters Breach while the other is above 50% Stagger, both take heavy shared damage and drop their barrier for 8 seconds. If only one is Breached for too long without the second being staggered, the first recovers and heals 10% HP.
- **Solution:** The party splits—one group focuses Heavy Attacks and Stagger tools on the left Sentinel while the other pressures the right Sentinel, coordinating a dual-Breach to drop the exit barrier and pull the twin release levers.

## 2. Final Boss: Orrery Warden Vaelis (The Clockwork Astrolabe)

### Boss Overview

Towering at the center of the Sunken Dome, Orrery Warden Vaelis is an ancient celestial automaton built from concentric brass astrolabe rings, a rotating crystal core, and a heavy gravity-halberd. Surrounded by shallow reflective water and four outer Astrolabe Pylons, Vaelis tests every facet of first-person awareness and Stagger management.

### Phase 1: The Turning Rings (100% – 65% HP)

**Mechanics:**

- **Astrolabe Sweep:** Vaelis swings its halberd in wide arcs that project glowing floor/water ripples—players can jump or Geyser-Flask over low sweeps, or use a Vanguard shield / timed block on directional strikes.
- **Orbiting Star-Shards:** Four crystalline shards orbit Vaelis, periodically firing homing Aether bolts. Shooting or tuning the shards drains their energy and adds Stagger to Vaelis.
- **First Breach Window:** Filling Vaelis's Stagger Meter forces the boss onto one knee, exposing its central Astrolabe Core for 4.5 seconds of bonus damage (+20%) and E Execute strikes.

### Phase 2: Eclipse Surge & Gravity Wells (65% – 30% HP)

**Mechanics:**

- At 65% HP, Vaelis ignites its outer rings and pulls nearby players slightly inward with a Gravity Vortex, spawning Blight-Spore hazard pools around the perimeter.
- **Vertical Escape:** Players must use Geyser-Flasks or Grapple Gauntlets to anchor onto elevated dome struts and Glider-Cloak across polluted water zones.
- **Interrupting Celestial Collapse:** Vaelis begins charging a room-wide shockwave (6-second cast bar). The party must hit two active Astrolabe Pylons with Resonance Tuning Forks or Heavy Attacks to stagger-interrupt the cast before it detonates.

### Phase 3: Shattered Zenith (30% – 0% HP)

**Mechanics:**

- Vaelis's outer brass rings crack and orbit faster, alternating between Cinder-Stalker / Breach-Trooper reinforcements and rapid Stagger Point rotations (left shoulder vs. right core).
- **Final Push:** Coordinated Breach chains keep Vaelis off balance through the final stretch, culminating in a dramatic collapse as the Observatory's overhead dome vents sunlight and bioluminescence into the chamber.

## 3. Loot Table & Delve Rewards

Completing The Sunken Observatory awards personal loot rolls (with anti-duplicate protection), Resonance Rank progress, and crafting materials for The Gloom-Basin.

### Boss & Final Chest Signature Gear

- **Astrolabe Pendulum Maul (Vanguard Weapon):** Heavy two-handed/shield-paired maul whose Heavy Attacks leave a brief slow-field on hit and deal +20% Stagger against constructs.
- **Star-Weave Conduit Staff (Arcanist Weapon):** Focus staff that grants +1 starting Aether Charge on entering combat and boosts beam Stagger damage.
- **Gale-Seeker Longbow (Waystalker Weapon):** Precision bow with faster full-draw speed while airborne or gliding.
- **Orrery Steam-Repeater (Artificer Carbine):** Carbine that vents a cooling burst on reload, reducing Geyser-Flask cooldown by 1.0s.

### Armor Pieces & Delve Currencies

**Observatory Set Pieces (2-Piece / 4-Piece Bonus):** Celestial Tide set drops (Helm, Mantle, Bracers, Greaves) offering bonus Stagger Resistance and Resonance Band charge speed in dungeons and Harmonic Convergences.

**Delve Currencies & Materials:**

- Sunken Star-Shards (used at The Brass-Root Yard in Cairn-Reach to socket or tune Level 10–15 weapons).
- Luminous Spore-Silk & Tempered Brass Ingots (used for Wayfinder Sanctum observatory lighting/furniture and Concordat Stronghold upgrades).

**Sanctum Trophy – Miniature Brass Orrery:** Rare drop that can be placed in a player's Wayfinder Sanctum as an animated tabletop display.
