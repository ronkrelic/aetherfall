# AETHERFALL: The Sunken Tiers - World & Lore

## Core Pitch

Set inside The Terraces of Aetherfall, a colossal stepped world where ancient sky-cities once tumbled into vast, sunlit canyons and glowing subterranean basins, players explore from a tactile first-person perspective. Every hand, tool, spell, and shield is physically visible in front of you - allowing you to grapple across chasms, vent pressurized steam to launch upward, read physical compass-glyphs on your wrist, and fight alongside dozens of other players in real time.

## 1. The World & Biomes

Aetherfall is structured into four interconnected vertical tiers, linked by natural waterfalls, wind-geysers, collapsed aqueducts, and player-built/guild-restored sky-lifts:

- **The Canopy Reach (Upper Tier):** Towering petrified sky-trees, hanging rope-bridges, windswept cloud-gardens, and sun-bleached watchtowers where wind currents carry gliders and spore-clouds.
- **The Amber Steppe (Mid-Upper Tier):** Golden grasslands dotted with fallen brass clockwork ruins, grazing herd beasts, and shifting magnetic dunes that tug lightly on metal gear.
- **The Gloom-Basin (Mid-Lower Tier):** Bioluminescent canyons, turquoise mineral rivers, cavern-mouths, and dense fungal groves that glow brightly at night and hide ancient shrines.
- **The Deep Foundry (Subterranean Tier):** High-heat geothermal vaults, humming resonator chambers, and crystal-lit chasms where the oldest machines of the world still run.

## 2. Complete Story & Lore

### The Era of the Seven Spires

Long ago, seven colossal Aether Spires anchored the sky-cities above the clouds, channeling raw atmospheric energy - called Aether - to shape stone, weather, and life. Civilization thrived not through fragile empires, but through Harmonies, grand civic projects where artists, engineers, and naturalists tuned the Spires together.

### The Cataclysm of the Quiet

Two centuries before the present day, a sudden resonance flaw - known as The Quiet - shattered the balance of the Spires. Without warning, five Spires fell, carving massive canyons into the continent while a strange stillness spread through the land. Magic grew erratic, ancient automata lost their original orders, and the sky became perpetually stormy above the highest peaks.

### The Present Day: The Re-Awakening

Today, the remaining two Spires are flickering back to life, sending pulses of golden and violet Aether across the Terraces. Rather than restoring peace, these pulses are waking dormant defenses, mutating wildlife, and drawing rival factions toward the ruins.

Players are Wayfinders - independent travelers, scavengers, and mercenaries who carry Resonance Bands on their wrists. Those bands let you sense Aether currents, unlock sealed vaults, and join massive open-world Harmonic Convergences (public events where dozens of players stabilize failing landmarks before corrupted surges overwhelm a region).

## 3. Factions: Good Guys, Bad Guys & Complicated Middles

### The Good Guys (and Hopeful Builders)

- **The Lantern Wardens:** A loosely organized network of rangers, healers, and scouts who keep trade trails safe, light beacon-pyres against night predators, and shelter displaced townsfolk.
- **The Brass-Root Guild:** Tinker-scholars and salvagers who repair ancient elevators, water wheels, and bridges for the public good - though they sometimes argue over whether to preserve old tech or repurpose it.

### The Bad Guys (and Major Threats)

- **The Ash-Crown Syndicate:** Ruthless industrialists and scavengers who harvest unstable Aether cores to power heavy siege engines and mercenary squads, caring little for the ecological damage left in their wake.
- **The Hollow Choir:** A fanatical cult that believes The Quiet was a sacred cleansing and tries to sabotage restoration efforts to force the world into permanent dormancy.
- **The Unbound Construct Hordes:** Ancient stone-and-amber guardians whose damaged logic-cores treat all living creatures as intruders.

## 4. NPCs & Enemies

### Notable NPCs

- **Quartermaster Mira Vance (Lantern Wardens):** A sharp-eyed scout captain in a sun-faded cloak who runs a clifftop waystation and sends players on dynamic escort, beacon-lighting, and supply runs.
- **Archivist Oakhaven (Brass-Root Guild):** An eccentric clockmaker-historian surrounded by ticking astrolabes and broken chimes, offering lore quests and gear-tuning upgrades.
- **Sable of the Low Tide:** A quiet river-ferryman and information broker who knows secret underwater passages and warns players of shifting canyon currents.

### Enemy Archetypes

- **Cinder-Stalkers:** Fast, pack-hunting predators adapted to volcanic and ash-strewn ridges; they flank in first-person combat and telegraph leaps with glowing eyes.
- **Amber Sentinels:** Towering stone-and-amber constructs with glowing fracture lines - breaking specific glowing joints staggers them and exposes their core.
- **Blight-Weavers:** Floating, spore-casting creatures in the Gloom-Basin that lay hazard zones and buff nearby corrupted beasts until interrupted.
- **Syndicate Breach-Troopers:** Armored human/faction enemies using shields, grappling lines, and Aether-charged rifles/crossbows that force players to use cover, movement, and crowd control.
