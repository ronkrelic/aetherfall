## 3. Enemy Bestiary

Each enemy entry specifies Tier Habitat, Attack Telegraphs, Stagger/Breach Weaknesses, and Signature Loot Drops so players can study and counter them in first-person combat.

### Cinder-Stalker (Pack Predator)

- **Habitat:** The Amber Steppe & volcanic canyon rims.
- **Behavior & Telegraphs:** Hunts in packs of 3–5. Circles the player's peripheral vision before crouching low—its eyes flare bright crimson 1.0s before a lunging bite.
- **Tactical Counter:** A timed Right-Click block during its pounce immediately fills 60% of its Stagger Meter and knocks it onto its side.
- **Notable Drops:** Scorched Sinew, Stalker Fang, Amber-Flecked Pelt.

### Blight-Weaver (Bioluminescent Spore-Caster)

- **Habitat:** The Gloom-Basin & fungal caverns.
- **Behavior & Telegraphs:** Hovers 3–4m above the ground, tethered by glowing mycelial tendrils. Pulses violet light before dropping toxic spore clouds and linking healing threads to nearby beasts.
- **Tactical Counter:** Use the Grapple Gauntlet to yank it down to ground level or hit its glowing central spore-sac with ranged/aerial attacks for instant Breach.
- **Notable Drops:** Luminous Spore-Gland, Gloom-Silk Thread, Runic Lantern Oil.

### Syndicate Breach-Trooper (Human Mercenary)

- **Habitat:** Sun-Split Quarry, Ash-Crown outposts, and contested ruin sites.
- **Behavior & Telegraphs:** Deploys collapsible iron tower shields and fires Aether-charged repeating rifles. Announces heavy volleys with a laser-like violet sightline 1.5s prior to firing.
- **Tactical Counter:** Flanking vertically via Geyser-Flask or shattering their shield with a Resonance Tuning Fork leaves them wide open to E Execute finishers.
- **Notable Drops:** Refined Brass Casings, Syndicate Cipher Tags, Unstable Aether Cells.

### Amber Sentinel (Ancient Construct – Elite / World Boss)

- **Habitat:** Ruin Vaults, the Singing Pillars, and the Deep Foundry.
- **Behavior & Telegraphs:** A towering stone-and-amber colossus wielding a seismic warhammer. Telegraphs ground-slam shockwaves with glowing orange runes racing down its arms (2.0s windup)—requiring players to jump or Geyser-Flask over the expanding ring.
- **Tactical Counter:** Features three independent Stagger Points (left knee, right knee, and chest core). Shattering both leg joints forces the Sentinel to kneel, exposing its chest core for full group Breach damage.
- **Notable Drops:** Resonant Amber Heart, Ancient Logic-Core, Spire-Breaker Schematics.
